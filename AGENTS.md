# Agent rules

Read `docs/architecture/REPO_LAYOUT.md` before adding files.

- The repo root holds entry points, config, containers, CI, docs, and the packages listed there. Do not add scratch files, logs, screenshots, or one-off probes beside `main.py`.
- `shared/` is the home for env getters, paths, logging, ffmpeg, image MIME, network retry, the durable channel store, the ContentItem contract, and the memory client. Import `shared`. `utils.pipeline_paths` is `shared.paths`, `utils.process_tools` is `shared.ffmpeg`, `modules.reel_artefact.net_retry` is `shared.retry`, `modules.durable_store` is `shared.store`, `modules.distribution_contract` is `shared.contracts`, and `modules.memory` is `shared.memory`. Do not add a second copy of those helpers. `channels_config/` keeps its name. The account registry will be a top-level `accounts/` package; do not add it under `distribution/` or rename `channels_config/` to create it.
- Secrets go in `.env` only. `.env.example` lists the names and stays empty. Never hardcode tokens, including test defaults.
- Video, audio, `*.npz`, `*.psd`, error screenshots, and `outputs/` stay out of git.
- Channel store state under `channels_config/<channel>/store/` stays gitignored and dockerignored, and only lives in the owner's Drive backup. `channels_config/aiwake/store/portfolio/` stays tracked and in the image. Files already tracked in `production/formats/lofi/store/` stay in git, and `aphorism_recent.json` stays gitignored (the old `core/economic_reel_lofi/store/` path stays ignored too). `.cursor/rules/` is versioned. `scene_library/`, `artefact_cache/`, and `reel_checkpoints/` under a channel store stay gitignored.
- Tests live in `tests/` or `channels_config/<channel>/tests/`. Name them for the behavior they lock, not for a smoke round number.
- Do not rename `agents/media/`, `config/pinterest_accounts.yaml`, `config/pinterest_image_map.yaml`, `channels_config/*/store`, `scene_library`, `artefact_cache`, the compose services `engine` and `scheduler`, or the `memory` profile. The worker entry is `python -m distribution.workers`. Publishers live in `distribution/publishers/`, scheduling in `distribution/scheduling/`, and the account registry in `accounts/`. The parametric animation engine lives in `animation/parametric/` (`animation/api.py` is the facade; the renderer stays ffmpeg). `core/animator/` keeps shims until M14. The Aiwake show and harness live in `products/aiwake/`; `channels_config/aiwake/` keeps the store and re-exports the show until M14. There is no `omni/` package. `run_main_noupload.py` stays a root entry point if it is added.
- `main.py` stays the pipeline CLI. `python tools/channel_cli.py --channel <id>` is the per-channel operator CLI. Persona sentences and `master_dna.json` stay in `channels_config/<channel>/`. The loader is `production.writers.persona.load_persona`. New pipeline steps go in `engine/` or the package that already owns that concern (`production/qa/`, `production/providers/`, `production/media/`, `production/writers/`, `distribution/publishers/`, `agents/media/`). Pin production lives in `production/formats/pins/`. Cost, including the per-reel cap, lives in `production/cost/`. Do not split root `config.py` in that move. The account registry is top-level `accounts/`, not `distribution/accounts/`.
- Temp files: `temp_path` / `review_path` in `shared/paths.py` (the `utils.pipeline_paths` shim is the same module). Logs: `outputs/logs/` via `pipeline_logs_dir()`.
