# Aiwake caption truth table

Claims in captions may use only the rows this table proves. A human-written category or opening axis seeds the orchestrator. Do not say "zero human input", "no human involvement", "fully autonomous", or "frontier".

| Fact | Answer | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Who writes the opening question: a human, verbatim, or the orchestrator LLM from a human-written category/prompt? | The orchestrator LLM writes the spoken question. A human-written bank seeds it. `pick_provocation_focus` chooses a `ProvocationFocus` whose `opening_directive` and rungs are fixed prose in `FOCUS_BANK`, or `pick_opening_dna` chooses a human-written `OpeningDNA` topic and directive. On a cold open the user stimulus is the fixed cue "Ask them. Now." The system brief tells the model the axis and forbids speaking the label. The first question must pass `is_valid_first_hook` (one question, at most 12 words). It is not a verbatim read of the human directive, and it is not an unseeded autonomous invention. | `channels_config/aiwake/provocations.py:54` `FOCUS_BANK`; `channels_config/aiwake/provocations.py:279` `pick_provocation_focus`; `channels_config/aiwake/provocations.py:369` `focus_brief`; `channels_config/aiwake/personas.py:261` `pick_opening_dna`; `channels_config/aiwake/personas.py:415` `is_valid_first_hook`; `channels_config/aiwake/orchestrator.py:226` `_provocation_stimulus`; `channels_config/aiwake/orchestrator.py:285` `provoke` |

| Is any dialogue text edited, trimmed, or re-rolled after generation? | Yes, by code, before the line is kept. `RoomConstraints.enforce` strips markup, drops prompt-echo sentences, and can drop an incomplete tail or extra sentences to meet the character, sentence, and word ceilings. ## Claims captions may use

- "unscripted replies"
- "no human wrote the replies"
- "both voices are AI"
- "AI-animated", "AI voices", "AI-generated"

## Claims captions may not use

- "zero human input", "no human involvement", "fully autonomous" (a human wrote the opening axis)
- "frontier" (the seats include Llama 3.3 70B and GPT-4o)
- "unedited" (the room may strip markup and trim at a sentence boundary before the line is kept)
- "zero human script", "zero script", "0% manual editing"
