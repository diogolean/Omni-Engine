# Aiwake caption style (v4)

Captions may claim only what `docs/caption_truth_table.md` allows. Never write "frontier", "zero human", "unedited", or "fully autonomous".

## Title

`<Asker> vs <Target> - <original opening question>`

- 80 characters maximum.
- Shorten only by rewording. Never drop words from the opening to make it fit.
- Names: Gemini, Llama, GPT-4o, DeepSeek, Claude.

## Body

1. Optional: one short line of context, 12 words maximum.
2. The punchline quote, verbatim, in straight double quotes, on its own line, 20 words maximum.
3. A concrete closing question about this clip's topic.
4. One true Unscripted/AI line.
5. Exactly 3 hashtags: one topic tag plus the model tags for the models in the clip.

TikTok caption length is 300 characters maximum, including the title, quote, question, Unscripted line, and hashtags.

Banned hashtags: `#AI`, `#Tech`, `#ArtificialIntelligence`, `#AIdebate`, `#Shorts`.

## Banned wording

Never use: "This exchange", "truly", "blunt", "delve", "the very nature", "what does that say about", or an em dash.

`admit*`, `corner*`, `confess*`, `collaps*`, `dodg*`, and `caught` are allowed only beside a verbatim `verdict_evidence` line from the transcript.

## Uniqueness

Closing questions are unique. Any two closers in the publishable set must have similarity below 0.7.

## Example

```
Claude vs GPT-4o - Strip out every stolen sentence. What's actually you?

GPT-4o keeps insisting it doesn't steal. Then Claude ends it:
"A parasite that borrows every word, then bills itself as the author."
Should AI companies pay the writers whose sentences trained their models?
Unscripted replies from both models, voiced by AI.

#AIethics #ChatGPT #Claude
```
